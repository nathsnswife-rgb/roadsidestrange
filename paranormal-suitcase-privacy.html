Paranormal Suitcase Privacy Policy

Effective: September 21, 2026 · Operator: DivineApps

This Privacy Policy explains how Paranormal Suitcase handles information when you use the Android app, including microphone recordings, camera images, device-sensor readings, internet-radio features, local investigation journals, and Google Play purchases.

1. Summary

Paranormal Suitcase is designed so that investigation recordings, photographs, sensor readings, notes, event markers, and journals are handled primarily on your device. DivineApps does not operate a server that receives or stores your investigation recordings, photographs, journal entries, or sensor-session files simply because you use the app.

2. Microphone and audio recordings

Paranormal Suitcase requests microphone access for features such as EVP recording, Room Watch, and Spirit Box session recording. Audio captured by the microphone is recorded and processed on the device. Raw recordings may be stored in the app's local investigation folders, and when you choose to export or save files, Android may place copies in device-accessible media or download locations. Audio enhancement creates a separate processed copy and does not intentionally upload the original or processed recording to DivineApps.

3. Camera and photographs

Paranormal Suitcase requests camera access for tools including the SLS Camera, Motion Camera, Evidence Camera, Sensor Overlay Camera, and optical portions of the REM Pod feature. Camera frames are processed on the device for the requested feature. Saved photographs may be written to your device's Pictures collection, including a Paranormal Suitcase folder where supported. DivineApps does not intentionally receive or store your photographs.

4. Device sensors

The app may read device sensors such as the magnetometer, accelerometer, compass/orientation sensors, and barometer when those sensors are available on your phone. These readings are used to operate tools such as K2 / EMF, Geophone, Anomaly Compass, Pressure Monitor, Sensor Overlay Camera, REM Pod, and Room Watch. Sensor readings and related event markers are processed locally and may be written into local investigation-session files. They are not intentionally transmitted to DivineApps.

5. SLS pose detection

The SLS Camera uses Google ML Kit pose-detection software to analyze visible-light camera frames and map detected body landmarks. The app's pose analysis is performed on the device. Paranormal Suitcase does not use the SLS tool to identify a person, recognize a face, or create a biometric identity profile.

6. Spirit Box and internet-radio connections

The Spirit Box can optionally connect to the Radio Browser directory and to third-party live internet-radio streams. When that feature is used, normal internet connection information such as your IP address, request headers, and device/network metadata may be visible to the directory service, the selected radio-stream provider, their hosting providers, or your network provider. Paranormal Suitcase logs station/source information locally with the investigation session so you can review the known audio source. DivineApps does not control the privacy practices of third-party radio services.

7. Investigation Journal and local files

Investigation sessions, recordings, event markers, source logs, and journal information are stored in app-specific storage on your device unless you intentionally export them. Some exported audio, images, or investigation files may be saved to Android media collections such as Music, Pictures, or Downloads. You control those files through your device and any backup or synchronization services you have enabled.

8. Premium purchases

Paranormal Suitcase uses Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Premium unlock. Google Play processes the purchase, payment information, account association, purchase token, and entitlement status according to Google's policies. The app queries Google Play to determine whether Premium is owned and stores a local cached entitlement value so access can remain available appropriately between checks. DivineApps does not receive your full payment-card details through the app.

9. Information stored in app preferences

The app may store local settings and state such as tester access mode in eligible test builds, Premium entitlement cache, tool settings, and other app preferences using Android local storage. These settings are used to operate the app and are not intentionally sent to DivineApps.

10. Advertising, analytics, accounts, and location

The current Android version of Paranormal Suitcase does not include third-party advertising SDKs, does not require a Paranormal Suitcase user account, does not include Firebase Analytics, and does not request device location permission. If those features change in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before or with that release.

11. Permissions

Depending on the tool you use, Paranormal Suitcase may request or use microphone, camera, internet, audio-system, and wake-lock capabilities. Optional hardware such as camera flash, compass/magnetometer, accelerometer, and barometer may also be used when available. You can deny or revoke Android runtime permissions through your device settings, although the affected tools may not function without the required permission.

12. Backups and third-party device services

Android or services configured on your device may back up app data or media according to your device, Google account, manufacturer, cloud-storage, or backup settings. Those services are outside DivineApps' direct control and are governed by their own privacy policies.

13. Data sharing and sale

DivineApps does not sell personal information collected through Paranormal Suitcase. The app does not intentionally share your locally recorded investigation audio, photographs, journal content, or sensor-session files with DivineApps or advertising companies. Data may be processed by Google Play when you use purchasing features and by third-party radio services when you choose to use live internet-radio functionality.

14. Retention and deletion

Because investigation data is stored primarily on your device, you can delete individual recordings, sessions, photographs, or exported files using the app where deletion is offered or through Android's file/media controls. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app can remove app-specific local data, subject to copies you previously exported and any device or cloud backups you control. Google Play retains purchase records according to Google's own legal and account-retention requirements.

15. Security

Paranormal Suitcase is designed to keep investigation content local to the device unless a feature specifically requires a network connection. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your phone, exported files, backups, and Google account.

16. Children

Paranormal Suitcase does not provide social networking, account creation, or a feature intended to collect identifying information from children. Users should not intentionally record or store sensitive personal information about another person without appropriate permission.

17. International users

Google Play and third-party internet-radio services may process technical or transaction information in countries where they or their service providers operate. Privacy protections and government-access rules can differ by country.

18. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Paranormal Suitcase features, permissions, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
