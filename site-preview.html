How do I find haunted places near me?

Roadside Strange uses a map and adjustable search area to surface reported hauntings, strange history and other unusual cases around the location you choose.

Can I build a haunted or paranormal road trip?

Roadside Strange includes road-trip tools for turning strange locations into stops, with directions and public-access information where available.

Can a phone work as an EMF meter?

Phones with a magnetometer can measure magnetic-field changes. Paranormal Suitcase uses supported hardware and explains environmental causes and limitations.

Does Paranormal Suitcase have thermal imaging?

No. It does not claim thermal imaging hardware that a standard phone does not have. The toolkit is built around supported phone sensors, camera, microphone and software.
