PARANORMAL SUITCASE

Weird is welcome. Made-up hardware isn’t.

Paranormal Suitcase is built for actual field use. It records, measures and organizes what your phone can genuinely detect, then explains what the reading means, what can create false positives and what it absolutely does not prove.

EVP Recorder

Capture microphone audio, mark interesting moments and scrub back through recordings during review.

K2 / EMF

Watch real magnetometer changes against a local baseline with familiar K2-style feedback.

Spirit Box

An experimental audio session tool with clear information about its source and limitations.

SLS Camera

Visible-light pose mapping using on-device pose detection, not imaginary infrared hardware.

Anomaly Compass

Record magnetic-field changes by direction during a deliberate 360° sweep.

Investigation Journal

Keep recordings, evidence, source logs and session markers together for later review.

Can a phone really measure EMF?

Phones with a magnetometer can measure magnetic-field changes. Paranormal Suitcase uses supported hardware and explains environmental causes and limitations.

Does Paranormal Suitcase fake thermal imaging?

No. If the phone does not have the hardware, the app does not pretend it does.
